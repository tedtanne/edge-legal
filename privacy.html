EDGE

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Noumena Holdings, LLC

The short version: everything you put into EDGE stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, we don't see it, and we don't track you.

You can export or delete all of it from inside the app, at any time.

Who we are

EDGE is a figure-skating app made by Noumena Holdings, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the EDGE app for iPhone.

What EDGE stores, and where

EDGE saves the following only on your device:

  • Skater profiles: the name you enter, discipline, level and, if you give it, a birth year. EDGE never asks for a full date of birth.
  • Scores you save, such as a fan score for a skate you watched, including the grades of execution you entered yourself.
  • App settings, such as whether you've finished setup.

This version of EDGE has no online accounts. The sign-up screens don't create an account with us, and an email address or password typed there is not saved or sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. EDGE does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Saving rules documents for offline use. In the Rules Library you can choose to save a rulebook document, such as an ISU Communication, so it opens without a connection at the rink. When you do, EDGE downloads that public document directly from its publisher's website and keeps it on your device. Nothing about you is sent with the request, but like any website, the publisher's server sees your device's IP address. EDGE only does this when you ask.

Apple, the App Store and TestFlight

Apple runs the App Store and TestFlight under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share crash reports with us. If you test EDGE through TestFlight, Apple shares your name, email address and any feedback or screenshots you send with us, and we use them only to improve EDGE and to contact you about testing.

Children

EDGE is used by skaters of every age. A skater under 18 is set up by a parent or guardian, who confirms their consent in the app. Because EDGE keeps everything on the device and sends nothing to us, we do not collect personal information from children. The profile stays on the family's device, under the guardian's control. EDGE has no public profiles, search or social features.

Your choices

  • Export: Profile → Account → Export My Data gives you a file of everything EDGE has stored.
  • Delete: Profile → Account → Delete Account removes everything EDGE has stored on the device. Deleting the app does the same.

Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's built-in data protection, which is tied to your passcode.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of EDGE sends any data off your device (for example, to sync between devices or to share with a coach), we will update this policy and tell you in the app before that version collects anything. The effective date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
